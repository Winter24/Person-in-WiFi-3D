RESFES 2026 — FINALE FULL-PAPER REGULATIONS
(Updated 2026-06-19 from the ResFes 2026 Organizing Committee email)

This file supersedes any earlier Preliminary-Round (proposal) regulations
for teams that have advanced to the Finale. The Preliminary-Round notes
are kept at the bottom for historical reference only.

================================================================================
1. FINALE — FULL-PAPER SUBMISSION
================================================================================

Deadline: 5.00 pm, 2 August 2026 (GMT+7, Indochina Time)
Submission channel: CMT only. Any full paper submitted through another
channel will NOT be considered for marking and evaluation.

Each research topic is registered under exactly one sub-committee. The
Organizing Committee will check eligibility and similarity for every
submission.

================================================================================
2. MANDATORY TEMPLATE
================================================================================

Full papers MUST follow the compulsory template provided by ResFes 2026:

- IT sub-committee — IEEE format:
    Word template:     <ResFes 2026 IEEE Word link>
    Overleaf template: <ResFes 2026 IEEE Overleaf link>
    Local copy:        Template/latexcode/ResFes_FE_Full paper_IEEE.tex
                       Template/latexcode/Bib_references.bib

- BA / CT / DAD / LIN sub-committees — APA format (7th edition):
    Word template:     <ResFes 2026 APA Word link>
    Overleaf template: <ResFes 2026 APA Overleaf link>
    Local copy:        Template/latexcode/ResFes_FE_Full paper_APA.tex

Project alignment: This repository targets the IT sub-committee. The
canonical local template is
`Template/latexcode/ResFes_FE_Full paper_IEEE.tex`, which uses
`\documentclass[conference,onecolumn]{IEEEtran}`. All page geometry,
margins, fonts, and spacing are inherited from IEEEtran defaults --
do not override.

Page limit: as defined by the IEEE conference template; teams should
observe the conference page limits noted in the template. (Where the
template is silent, follow IEEE's standard for conference papers and
keep core content within the typical IEEE conference range.)

================================================================================
3. CITATION & REFERENCING
================================================================================

- IT sub-committee:                   IEEE format
- BA / CT / DAD / LIN sub-committees: APA format (7th edition)

================================================================================
4. LANGUAGE
================================================================================

All written and oral content must be in English.

================================================================================
5. PLAGIARISM (TURNITIN)
================================================================================

Turnitin similarity index must NOT exceed 25%. Teams whose similarity
index exceeds 25% are disqualified. Run a Turnitin check before
submitting.

================================================================================
6. DISQUALIFICATION CRITERIA (FINALE)
================================================================================

Failure to satisfy any single one of the following disqualifies the
team from the Finale:

- Submission after the 2026-08-02 17:00 (GMT+7) deadline
- Submission via any channel other than CMT
- Departure from the compulsory ResFes 2026 IEEE/APA template
- Citation/reference style not matching the assigned sub-committee
- Paper not written in English
- Turnitin similarity index > 25%

================================================================================
7. ON-SITE PRESENTATION (Finale)
================================================================================

Finalist teams must prepare and submit:
- Full research paper (this document covers it)
- Presentation slides (mandatory; free-style format)
- Other visual aids (optional)

Presentation time per team: 30 minutes
  - 10-minute presentation
  - 20-minute Q&A

Venue: FPT University, Can Tho Campus, Vietnam.

================================================================================
SOURCE
================================================================================
